## What Online Shopping Rewards Really Are

Online shopping rewards, in plain terms, are a slice of the commission a merchant pays to whoever sends them a paying customer. Comparison sites, deal blogs, influencer links, and browser-extension cashback apps all sit in that referral chain. A cashback platform registers as one of those referrers, earns a commission from the merchant when you buy, and shares most of that commission back to you as real money.

Three rules fall out of that arrangement:

1. **You pay the same price at checkout.** Cashback comes from the merchant's affiliate budget, not a markup on your order.
2. **The referral has to be tracked.** You have to click through the cashback platform first so the merchant attributes the visit. No click-through, no commission, no cashback.
3. **Cashback clears after the merchant validates the order.** A refund or return reverses it; the return window has to close first.

Everything else — pending vs confirmed timing, category exclusions, why some rates hit double digits — flows from those three points. The honest framing matters because it sets realistic expectations: online shopping rewards are not "free money" in a magical sense, they are a structural rebate you activate by changing where your shopping session starts.

## Tracking Tips to Make Sure Your Cashback Lands

The most common frustration with online shopping rewards is a purchase that did not track. The fix is mostly mechanical.

- **Always start your session at ShopBack.** The click-through is what creates the referral. Bookmark the site or keep the app on your home screen.
- **Disable ad-blockers and cashback-blockers** on the merchant's checkout page. They can strip the tracking cookie.
- **Clear competing affiliate cookies** before starting, or use a fresh browser session. Multiple cashback links in the same session can cancel each other out.
- **Avoid coupon-site detours** between clicking through from ShopBack and completing checkout. Use promo codes listed on ShopBack itself, which are merchant-approved and cashback-compatible.
- **Do not switch devices mid-checkout.** Start and finish on the same browser or app session.
- **File a missing-cashback claim** through the ShopBack app (Account → Cashback → Missing Cashback) within the claim window stated on the merchant's page if a purchase does not track. The platform can often recover it.

These are not edge cases — they are the difference between online shopping rewards that reliably land and online shopping rewards that quietly vanish.
